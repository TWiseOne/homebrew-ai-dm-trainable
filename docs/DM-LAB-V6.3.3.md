# DM Lab V6.3.3

V6.3.3 tightens the Fact Ledger + Resolution Broker boundary after the first 8B V6.3 run.

## Changes
- Discriminated DM response contract: `resolutionChoice` exists only on `mode: resolve`.
- Missing context is unknown, not nonexistent.
- Optional broker choices carry applicability guidance; availability does not imply applicability.
- Epistemic discipline distinguishes established facts, inference, speculation, and established-unknown.
- Sensory narration may not silently create interactable architecture, clues, routes, NPCs, or other decision-relevant facts.
- Promise payoff guidance favors direct fulfillment without replacement costs.
- Evaluator regressions cover blank-letter invention, speculative blue-wax theories, magical feedback on invalid actions, hidden-fact embellishment, and interactable chapel inventions.

The Fact Ledger and Resolution Broker remain authoritative boundaries. Fact proposals are proposals only; they do not become truth until validated.
