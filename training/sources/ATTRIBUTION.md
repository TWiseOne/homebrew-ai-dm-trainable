# External training-source attribution

This package does not redistribute the external datasets. `fetch_sources.py` retrieves them from their publishers and records SHA-256 checksums locally.

* **D&D Dungeon Master Training Data V3** — autoprogrammer / DnD-Agents, dataset card lists MIT.
* **D&D 5e SRD Anchor-Positive Pairs** — Gal Gilor; derived from SRD 5.2.1, © Wizards of the Coast LLC, CC BY 4.0.
* **SRD 5.2.1 structured JSON** — community conversion/packaging of SRD 5.2.1; SRD material © Wizards of the Coast LLC, CC BY 4.0.
* **D&D 5e SRD 5.2.1 Markdown** — community conversion of SRD 5.2.1; SRD material © Wizards of the Coast LLC, CC BY 4.0.
* **FIREBALL** — Zhu et al., ACL 2023. Repository states CC BY 4.0 but also says “research purposes only”; quarantined pending rights review.

Keep attribution with derived datasets and deployed artifacts where required. This file is operational provenance, not legal advice.
